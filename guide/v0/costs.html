Costs and Guardrails

The menu bar

The menu bar icon shows how many MicroVMs are running and roughly what they cost an hour. Its menu lists each MicroVM with its state, time left and cost, and offers Connect, Save Work to a WIP Branch and Terminate…. It refreshes every minute.

The estimate covers compute only, for running MicroVMs, from each image's size at Lambda MicroVM rates; see Images. Suspended MicroVMs count as nothing. EFS, NAT gateways and data transfer aren't included.

Warnings

  • Time left: a notification 30 minutes and 5 minutes before a MicroVM stops, with Save Work and Open.
  • Idle: a notification when a MicroVM has had no shell for a while, with Terminate and Open. Choose how long, or Never, in Settings → General → Notifications. MicroVMs launched with Suspend when idle off are left out: the menu lists them as Working unattended.

Warnings are notifications, so allow MicroVM Studio's notifications in System Settings to see them.

Save Work

For MicroVMs MicroVM Studio launched from a workspace with repos. For each repo, it pushes your staged, unstaged and untracked changes, and any unpushed commits, to a new branch on origin named wip/<session>-<date>-<time> (UTC). Your working tree and branch don't change. A suspended MicroVM is resumed for the save, then suspended again.

Quitting

Quitting while MicroVMs that MicroVM Studio launched are running lists them: tick the ones to stop, then Terminate Selected and Quit, or Keep All and Quit. The ones you keep go on running, and billing.
